g sSearch g tTimeline. With the chart focused, h l move by month, H L by year, v marks a range (or drag across the bars), > narrows to it. Scroll or + - to zoom; shift-scroll, shift-drag or middle-drag to pan; Z or double-click shows every month. g rGraph (g n too), for exploring how tags connect: tags that appear together, tags related in meaning, or entities that appear together — T cycles. Thicker, darker lines are stronger links. With the chart focused, h l step through nodes (orange = cursor), w follows the cursor's strongest link (2w the second, as numbered under the graph), b steps back, space picks the cursor node, > narrows to the picked nodes (any of them) or else the cursor node, Esc clears the picks, then the preview, + - zoom, 0 resets. Nothing is orange until you move or tap: an untouched graph has no special node. Moving the cursor previews its items below (the dashed chip) without narrowing. Click a node to preview it, shift-click (or ctrl-click) to pick it, double-click to narrow; scroll to zoom, drag to pan, double-click empty space to reset. By touch: one finger pans, two pinch to zoom, a tap follows a node, and a long press picks one or drops it again — the line under the graph says how many are picked, and the pick · clear · narrow row under it does the same by thumb. g mMap of the whole archive by meaning; your scope lights up. With the chart focused, h l step through topics (T for themes), z zooms to one, + - zoom, 0 resets, space picks the cursor topic (orange), > narrows to the picked topics and netted dots (or the cursor topic), Esc clears them, f finds a tag or entity (arrows preview it, Enter narrows). Nothing is orange until you move or tap. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan, click a dot to open it, shift-click (or ctrl-click) a dot or label to pick its topic, shift-drag to net the dots inside. Close in, dots show their titles. By touch: one finger pans, two pinch to zoom, a tap opens the dot or moves the cursor to a label, and a long press picks that topic or drops it again. The net · pick · clear · narrow row under the map does the rest: net turns one-finger drag into the lasso until you tap it again (Esc too), and clear counts what is picked.
